📦 3. Detailed Feature Scope by Module

Functional Specs

🗄️ Module A: Core CRM & Customization

Core Engine
  • Dynamic Schema Customization: Enables administrators to define custom data fields (text, dropdowns, dates, stages) on the fly utilizing PostgreSQL JSONB structures.
  • 360° Contact Management: Unified profiles storing lead demographic data, purchase intent, and multi-channel activity history.
  • Visual Kanban Pipelines: Drag-and-drop opportunity management with custom deal stages, win probabilities, and value calculations.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Granular permissions separating Super Admins, Sales Managers, and Frontline Agents.

💬 Module B: Omnichannel AI Communications

AI Layer
  • Unified Agent Inbox: Single multi-threaded workspace handling emails, WhatsApp messages, and Instagram DMs.
  • AI Chat Agents (WhatsApp & Instagram):
    • Autonomous FAQ handling, lead qualification, and meeting scheduling.
    • Automated fallback and human handoff on negative sentiment detection.
  • AI Voice Calling Infrastructure:
    • Inbound: AI Virtual Receptionist to qualify leads and log transcript summaries.
    • Outbound: Event-driven voice calls triggered post-form submissions.

📊 Module C: Marketing Automation & Live Ad Dashboards

Growth Engine

📈 Live Paid Ads Intelligence

  • Read-only API synchronizations with Meta, Google, and LinkedIn Marketing APIs.
  • Aggregated dashboards displaying Live Spend, Cost Per Click (CPC), Leads, and ROAS.
  • Hourly synchronization via Redis-managed background cron jobs.

📱 Social Publishing & Drip Automation

  • Multi-platform social content scheduler for LinkedIn and Meta pages.
  • Trigger-based WhatsApp/Email drip marketing sequences based on pipeline progression.
